# WatchOutTeacher icon

A watchful eye with a W-shaped lower edge: charcoal, warm white and amber.

`icon-v1.png` is the approved source artwork, generated with ImageGen. The original prompt is in `prompt-v1.txt`. Production PNGs in `extension/icons/` are resized copies for the toolbar, extension manager and settings page.

Keep the icon free of added lettering and give it enough space to remain readable at small sizes.
